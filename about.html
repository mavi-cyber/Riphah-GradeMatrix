How SGPA & CGPA are Calculated

Student academic performance is evaluated through Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) for a single semester and Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) across all completed semesters. According to the university regulations, the calculation works as follows:

1. Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA)

SGPA is calculated by dividing the total quality points earned in a semester by the total semester credit hours (excluding courses with grades 'W' and 'I'):

SGPA = ∑ (Course Credit Hours × Grade Points Earned) / Total Semester Credit Hours

2. Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA)

CGPA reflects overall academic standing across all enrolled semesters, calculated by dividing total quality points by total credit hours taken (excluding 'W', 'I', and 'R' grades):

CGPA = ∑ (Course Credit Hours in all semesters × Grade Points Earned) / Total Credit Hours taken

3. Key Calculation Rules & Rounding

  • Non-Credit Courses: Non-credit courses are completely excluded from both SGPA and CGPA calculations.
  • Marks Rounding: Marks for each course are rounded off only once after combining in-semester and final exam marks. If the first decimal is 5 or higher, it rounds up; if less than 5, it rounds down.
  • GPA Rounding: Both SGPA and CGPA are officially rounded off to two decimal places on transcripts and records.
