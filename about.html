Grading policy

How the numbers work

The rules this calculator follows, taken from the RIU Academic Regulations for Undergraduate Programs.

Which students this covers: the 5th Revision (2024, version 2.1) of the regulations applies to students admitted from the Fall 2023 intake onward. Earlier batches are governed by the regulations of their own intake, which may differ.

The scale

Student performance is evaluated through quizzes, assignments, mid-term examinations, projects, and final examinations. Your total marks land in one of these bands.

Within a band, the grade point increases by 0.1 for every 1 score above the minimum for the letter grade.

How SGPA & CGPA are calculated

Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) covers a single semester. Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) covers all completed semesters.

1

Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA)

Total quality points earned in a semester divided by the total semester credit hours (excluding courses with grades 'W' and 'I').

SGPA = ∑ (Course Credit Hours × Grade Points Earned) / Total Semester Credit Hours

2

Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA)

Overall academic standing across all enrolled semesters: total quality points divided by total credit hours taken (excluding 'W', 'I', and 'R' grades).

CGPA = ∑ (Course Credit Hours in all semesters × Grade Points Earned) / Total Credit Hours taken

3

Key calculation rules & rounding

  • Non-Credit Courses: Non-credit courses are completely excluded from both SGPA and CGPA calculations.
  • Marks Rounding: Marks for each course are rounded off only once after combining in-semester and final exam marks. If the first decimal is 5 or higher, it rounds up; if less than 5, it rounds down.
  • GPA Rounding: Both SGPA and CGPA are officially rounded off to two decimal places on transcripts and records.

Repeating a course and the R grade

You may repeat a course in which you got D, F or W. Both attempts stay on the transcript, and the rule below decides which one counts.

  • Substitute elective: a failed elective replaced by a different one, with the Dean's approval, becomes R in the same way.

Standing, honours and limits

The calculator checks these after each semester and flags them on the matrix.

!

Academic deficiency

  • A minimum CGPA of 2.0 must be kept throughout.
  • After a regular semester, a CGPA below 2.0 adds one probation. Reaching 2.0 again clears the count.
  • Three probations in a row mean relegation: only repeatable courses can be registered until the CGPA is back to 2.0.
  • First semester: a GPA below 1.0 means dismissal. From 1.0 to below 1.5 is a serious warning, and the next semester GPA must reach 2.0.
★

Honours

  • Vice Chancellor's List: SGPA of 3.7 or more in a regular semester, with no repeated course.
  • Dean's List: SGPA of 3.5 or more in a regular semester, with no repeated course.
  • Gold medals: the highest CGPA in the batch and at least 3.5, grade C or above in every course, and no repeated course, withdrawal or I grade.
#

Credit hours and special cases

  • A regular semester is 9 to 18 credit hours, or up to 21 with approval when that completes the degree. A summer session is at most 8.
  • W: withdrawal, or attendance below 75%. No credit, not used in SGPA or CGPA.
  • I: incomplete. Not used in SGPA or CGPA, and it turns into F if not completed by the end of the next regular semester.
  • Non-credit and transferred courses appear on the transcript but are not used in SGPA or CGPA.
  • Relative grading: a class of more than 20 students may be graded against the class average. Then enter the letter you were given instead of marks.
